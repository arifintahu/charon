## Patterns

- **SL dominates: 77% of exits hit -10% stop, averaging -36.61% slippage past trigger.** Real fill is well below the -10% trigger — 5-min candles allow tokens to gap straight through the stop. Tight stops on illiquid memecoins aren't actually stopping at -10%.
- **The 30 trailing-TP winners (+98.97% avg, 108 min avg hold) more than cover loss volume — but not loss magnitude.** Wins average ~99%, losses average ~37%; you need a ~27% win rate just to break even at those magnitudes. Current 22.9% win rate puts the strategy net negative.
- **LLM confidence does not separate winners.** 80+ bucket: 25% win rate, +6.47% avg (n=4, too small). 70-79 bucket: 22.2% win rate, -7.74% avg (n=126). Lifting `llm_min_confidence` would gut volume without improving edge.

## Recommendations

1. **Stop loss — investigate slippage past trigger.** SL trades close at -36.61% avg vs -10% trigger; that's 26pp of slippage. Either widen `sl_percent` to -25 (acknowledge gaps and stop fighting them, giving room for the trailing TP to develop) or tighten `POSITION_CHECK_MS` and intra-bar trigger logic to catch -10% on the way down. Validate with `npm run backtest -- --strategy degen --from 7d --override-sl -25` before shipping.
2. **Win rate target — raise filter selectivity, not LLM threshold.** Need ≥27% win rate just to break even at current magnitudes. LLM buckets show no edge, so push selectivity at the candidate-gate layer instead: raise `min_mcap_usd` (drift cohort showed lower min_mcap=15k correlates with -38% avg) or tighten `TRENDING_MIN_SWAPS` / `TRENDING_MIN_VOLUME_USD`. Sweep with `npm run backtest -- --from 7d --spec sweep.json`.
3. **Trailing TP — confirm 15% trail isn't capping winners early.** Winners average +99% with 108-min hold, suggesting the 30% TP / 15% trail combo lets runners breathe. Worth a sweep at `trailing_percent ∈ {10, 15, 20, 25}` to verify 15 is optimal vs alternatives.

## Code changes applied

Applied on 2026-05-16 22:33 +0700 in response to this evaluation. Root cause: 14 of the SL trades in the past 3d closed at ≤-70% (clear rugpulls). Inspection of the snapshots showed `candidate.trending.rug_ratio` and `bundler_rate` are both `null` for every rug — the Jupiter trending source (`TRENDING_SOURCE=jupiter`) hardcodes `rug_ratio: null` at `src/enrichment/jupiter.js:56`, and the filter check at `src/pipeline/candidateBuilder.js:42-43` defaults null → 0, which passes. Strategy carried `trending_max_rug_ratio: 0.5` and `trending_max_bundler_rate: 0.7` settings that have never gated a single trade. The bot has been silently flying without rug protection since the rug filters were added.

Wired up 3 filters using signals that *are* populated by Jupiter trending: `smart_degen_count` (organic buyer count, 0 on 11/14 rugs), `hot_level` (organic score, 0 on the same 11), `top_10_holder_rate` (concentration, would catch 2 additional rugs at threshold 0.4). Combined these would have caught ~13/14 outright rugs.
