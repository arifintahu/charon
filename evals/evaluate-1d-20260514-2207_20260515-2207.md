## Recommendations

1. **Tighten entry filters before touching exits** — 21/137 win rate at avg entry mcap below 100k means the candidate funnel is pulling too many fade-outs. Raise `degen` `min_mcap_usd` back to 15000 (post-change cohort is only 10 trades, not enough to justify 5000) and consider adding a swap/volume floor via `TRENDING_MIN_SWAPS` / `TRENDING_MIN_VOLUME_USD`. Goal: lift win rate from 15% toward 25%+.
2. **Investigate SL slippage** — actual SL fills (-34.7% avg) are 2.3x the configured -15% trigger. Check `POSITION_CHECK_MS` cadence and whether `exitSimulator.evaluateExitTick` is firing on stale candle data for the lowest-liquidity tokens. If real-trigger fills are inherent to this mcap band, the filter (rec #1) is the lever, not a tighter SL.
3. **Re-examine LLM confidence as a gate** — the 80+ bucket went 0/5 and the 60-69 bucket went 0/2; only the 70-79 mass produced wins. Either raise `llm_min_confidence` and accept fewer trades, or stop using confidence as a gate at all on `degen` and rely purely on filters. Run a backtest sweep before changing live config: `npm run backtest -- --from 7d --override-llm-min-confidence 75` and compare.
4. **Don't widen the trailing** — the +107% trailing avg with 89% stddev says winners run hard; trail=10 vs trail=15 in this 10-trade post-change cohort is statistically meaningless. Hold `trailing_percent` at 15 until rec #1 pushes win rate up enough that loosening is worth testing.
