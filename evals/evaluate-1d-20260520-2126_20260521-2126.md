## Code changes applied — 2026-05-21

Applied in response to the 8-day trend comparison showing a regression on 2026-05-20 (win rate 24.7%, down from 30.6%) introduced by `sl_arm_delay_ms: 30000`, plus follow-on improvements targeting the persistent SL slippage gap.

Key finding from reading `src/execution/exitSimulator.js`: `tp_percent` only fires the hard TP exit when `trailingEnabled = false`. With `trailing_enabled: true` and `trailing_arm_percent > 0`, `tp_percent` is a dead parameter for degen. The effective lever is `trailing_arm_percent`.

### Why trailing_arm_percent: 12 (not tp_percent)

`tp_percent` never triggers an exit when `trailing_enabled: true` — the exit evaluator only checks `tpHit && !trailingEnabled`. Lowering `tp_percent` has zero effect. Lowering `trailing_arm_percent` from 15 to 12 means:
- Trail arms earlier (+12% vs +15%)
- For trades that spike +12% and fall: trail locks at 1.12 × 0.85 = -4.8% (vs SL at -8%)
- Converts the ~5% of SL trades that touched +12–15% from full SL losses to partial trail exits
