No backtest gate, per the tune-loop convention.

### Recommendations 1 & 3 — discarded (false alarms)

**Rec #3 (LLM screener bug) — no change.** `degen` runs `use_llm: false`, so the LLM never executes. `src/pipeline/orchestrator.js` (the `if (!strat.use_llm)` branch, ~L47-57) hardcodes `confidence: 100` for every rule-based decision that clears the filters. The confidence=100 constant is the intended sentinel ("passes any `llm_min_confidence` gate"), not a saturated or broken verdict. Nothing to fix in `src/pipeline/llm.js`; a confidence gate on degen would be inert.

**Rec #1 (tighten entry floors) — no change.** `trending_min_swaps` (100) and `trending_min_volume_usd` (5000) are already active strategy-JSON fields; they read null only in the `env` snapshot. The rug / instant-dump tail was investigated on 2026-05-26 (7d, 784 trades) and is unfilterable on every captured signal — a priced-in cost, not an entry-gate problem.
