## Trailing-percent sweep + config change (2026-06-02)

Rec #1 (sweep trail before touching it) was run, because the backtester became usable on 05-31 (SOL→USD candle-unit fix) and the trail lever had whipsawed 15→18→15 without resolution.

**Setup:** `npm run backtest -- --from 7d --spec evals/sweep-trailing-percent.json --interval 1_MINUTE --candle-rule pessimistic --top 10`. Spec swept `trailing_percent ∈ {12,15,18,21,24}` (extended from {12,15,18,21} to cover the looser end the eval hypothesized). Warmed the 1m USD candle cache first — only 137/997 degen mints had 1m USD candles (the 05-31 quote fix orphaned the old `native` rows); fetch inserted 565,443 candles → 995/996 coverage. Read-only against remote Postgres.

**Validation (trust dial):** 88.8% exit-reason match, 45.3% within ±5% PnL (n=490; ±15%: 78.8%). The strict ≥80%/≥90% per-trade gate fails on the documented candle-resolution ceiling (degen tokens wick to the -10% SL intra-minute then recover; 1m lows model that as a hard stop, live's ~5s polling rides through). Not a defect for a *relative* trail comparison.

**Per-cell results (~1,160 closed sims/cell, all ≫ 10-trade floor):**

| **12** | 38.1 | **+1.51** | **+0.8763** | 457 / +19.31 | 707 / -10.00 | 7.0 |
| **15 (was live)** | 34.5 | +0.98 | +0.5715 | 436 / +19.30 | 727 / -10.00 | 7.7 |

**Ranking (avg PnL and net SOL agree): 12 > 24 > 15 > 18 > 21.** Monotonic decline 12→21: loosening converts winners into losers (TRAILING_TP 457→391, SL 707→772). Per-winner upside *does* grow with a looser trail (+19.3% → +27.1%), so trail=15 genuinely clips the right tail — but clipping is +EV: the wider trail gives back more on reversals than the fatter survivors recover. trail=24's non-monotonic uptick is a few lottery survivors with the worst win rate (26.2%), deepest drawdown, and most ambiguous candles — not a contender. All cells' ambiguity is under the 20% noise band, so the ranking isn't inside the optimistic/pessimistic band.

**Decision:** the eval's "loosen the trail" hypothesis is backwards — tightening is the +EV direction. Applied `trailing_percent` 15 → 12.

## smart_money-universe pivot on degen (2026-06-02)

Follow-up to a strategic question — "is the method wrong, trading early memecoin?" Diagnosis: of the four strategy files, only `degen` runs, and it's the weakest bet — fresh graduates (≤1h), public micro-filters, `use_llm: false`, no maturity/distribution/skin-in-game gate. The three strategies that encode an actual edge thesis (`sniper`, `smart_money`, `dip_buy`) are all disabled and untested. Decision: port `smart_money`'s *selection* gates onto `degen`, keep LLM off, accept lower volume for (hoped) higher quality.

### Evidence — holder-count is a real PnL discriminator (7d, 712 closed degen trades)

degen never gated on holders (`min_holders: 0`), so entry holder-count varied freely — a clean natural experiment:

| Entry holders | n | Win% | Avg PnL% | Net SOL | TRAILING_TP avg |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1000+** | 82 | **43.9** | **+1.14** | **+0.047** | +16.8% |
| 500–999 | 306 | 31.0 | -0.36 | -0.055 | +20.4% |
| 200–499 | 286 | 27.3 | **-3.21** | **-0.459** | +21.1% |
| <200 | 38 | 42.1 | +0.98 | +0.019 | +35.0% |

The ≥1000-holder band is the only meaningful-sized band that's net-positive (44% win, +1.14% avg). The 200–999 middle (83% of volume) carries the bleed. Feed feasibility (7d, 6,597 candidates): holder-count populated on 100% of rows, 1,319 (20%) at ≥1000 holders, `top20 ≤ 50` passes 99% — so the gate is viable (not a zero-trade trap) and ~inert on concentration.

### Caveats & what to watch

- **Holder gate is evidence-backed but thin & observational** — +0.047 SOL over 82 trades in one 7d window. Forward-test, not a proven edge.
- **Age/mcap widening is unvalidated** — degen never traded outside 1h / 50–100k, so there's no in-sample read on older or out-of-band tokens. Lowering the mcap floor to 10k runs against the 05-30 gradient (sub-50k was net-negative), though the ≥1000-holder requirement may neutralize it (those losers had few holders). If sub-50k entries show up negative in the next eval, re-raise `min_mcap` to 50000 to isolate the holder gate.
- Volume direction is uncertain: `min_holders 1000` cuts to ~20% of the old feed, but widening age/mcap adds tokens back — net could land either way.
- Attribution stays clean — the entry snapshot captures the new config, so the next `/evaluate` auto-splits the pre/post-change cohorts (same as the 05-26 mcap change). Watch whether the post-change cohort holds ~40%+ win / net-positive.
