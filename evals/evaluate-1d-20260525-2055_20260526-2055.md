### Rug-cohort signal-separation dig (7d, 784 closed degen trades)

To decide whether *any* dormant entry gate could screen the dump tail, pulled entry-time snapshot signals split into three cohorts:

| Cohort | Definition | n | avg PnL |
|---|---|---|---|

| RUG | SL & (pnl < -25 OR hold < 60s) | 189 | -20.6% (min -97.6%) |

Result — **no captured signal separates RUG from WIN:**

| Gate / signal | Verdict |
|---|---|
| `trending_min_liquidity_change_5m` | Backwards — RUG has a *fatter* positive tail than WIN (p90 32.9 vs 24.9). Raising the floor blocks winners first. |
| `min_mcap_usd` | Entry mcap statistically identical across all 3 cohorts (~61k median). Tail is NOT low-mcap; raising 50k→higher won't thin it. |
| `top_10_holder_rate` | Weak — best threshold (>0.30) blocks 13% of rugs at a 6% winner cost; leaves 87% of the tail. |
| `top20Percent`, `bot_degen_rate`, gmgn bundler %, `smart_degen_count`, `holderCount` | RUG ≈ WIN, or RUG *cleaner* than WIN — a max/min gate cuts winners as fast as rugs. |
| `trending_max_rug_ratio`, `trending_max_bundler_rate` | **Inert** — `null` in 100% of snapshots; the trending feed never populates them, so the gate value is irrelevant. |

### Exit side already settled (May 17–18 eval)

Faster polling / tighter stop don't help either: at `POSITION_CHECK_MS=5000` the monitor catches rugs in 4–9 polls, but fills land at near-zero liquidity (atomic LP pull). Tighter `sl_percent` gaps through (May 23–24: -8 produced *worse* fills than -10); `sl_arm_delay_ms` was tried May 20 and reverted May 21 as a regression.

### Decision: hold — no config change

At buy time rugs are indistinguishable from winners on every recorded signal, and by the time a dump registers the LP is gone. Entry gates, mcap floor, poll cadence, and stop placement are now all checked and all dead ends. The rug tail is a **priced-in cost** — the strategy is net-positive because the winners (avg +23%, up to +338%) pay for it.

Remaining honest levers (none shipped this session):
- `position_size_sol` / `max_open_positions` — reduces SOL-at-risk, not % expectancy (a risk-appetite call).
- Capturing a *new* signal the bot doesn't record (deployer history, real-time depth) — a feature build, not a config tune; likely not worth it while the strategy is green.

**Action: change nothing; confirm the positive flip (first net-positive day in the 14-day dataset, but breakeven cleared by only 0.3pp) over 2–3 more windows. Do not re-recommend entry filters for the rug tail.**
