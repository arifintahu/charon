## Patterns

- Stop-loss is the dominant exit at 59.9% of trades, with an avg PnL of -19.30% and a 2.2-minute average hold — the bot is being stopped out fast and hard on the majority of entries.
- TRAILING_TP exits average +24.16% over 9.3 minutes, indicating the upside when tokens do run is meaningful, but winners (40.1%) do not offset losers (59.9%) in frequency.
- The 4 trades under the older min_mcap=30000 config averaged +8.40% vs -2.12% for the 158 trades under min_mcap=40000, suggesting the mcap floor increase may not have improved quality — though the sample of 4 is too small to conclude.

## Recommendations

1. **Tighten SL or widen it with a trail arm** — 97 SL exits at avg -19.30% with 2.2-min hold suggests the bot enters and immediately hits the stop. Either the -10% SL is too tight for the token volatility in this mcap band, or entries are timed badly. Consider raising SL to -15% or -20% to give positions more room, combined with a tighter trailing arm to lock gains faster once a token runs.

2. **Review min_mcap_usd reversion** — The 4 trades at min_mcap=30000 outperformed (avg +8.40% vs -2.12%). Run a backtest sweep over mcap floors (25k, 30k, 40k, 50k) to check if the 40k floor is filtering out better candidates or just reducing volume.

3. **Raise LLM min_confidence floor** — 98.8% of trades (160/162) are at confidence 80+, meaning the LLM filter is barely discriminating. The 2 trades in the 70-79 bucket both lost (avg -45.60%). Consider setting `llm_min_confidence` to 82 or 85 to sharpen signal quality, or investigate whether the LLM is systematically overconfident.

4. **Investigate 2.2-minute SL holds** — Trades stopped out in ~2 minutes may be entering at a local top or during a pump exhaust. Add entry timing filters (e.g. requiring a cooldown after a large candle) to reduce chasing behavior.

5. **Check TRAILING_TP arm threshold** — Winners average 9.3 min to exit via TRAILING_TP. If the trail is arming too late or at too loose a threshold, some of that +24.16% avg gain may be given back before locking. Review `trailing_arm_pct` vs actual peak PnL on TRAILING_TP trades.

## Next-Day Recommendations

Based on the 6-day trajectory, the current config is close to breakeven. The following are ordered by expected impact.

### 1. Lower trailing arm threshold to 12% (test)

The trail arms at 15%, locking at ~+15% exit. SL hold time collapsed to 2.2 min, meaning most losers are exiting on quick fades — not late dump-and-recover cycles. Lowering `trailing_arm_percent` to 12 would arm the trail on more of those 2-min trades that briefly touch +10-14% before fading. Risk: more premature locks at smaller gains. Validate with `npm run backtest -- --from 7d --override-trailing-arm 12` before shipping.

### 2. Retest min_mcap_usd at 30k vs 40k

The 4 pre-change trades at min_mcap=30000 averaged +8.40% vs -2.12% at 40000. Sample is too small to conclude, but this has appeared across two consecutive windows as a potential signal. Run a sweep: `npm run backtest -- --from 7d --spec sweep.json` with `min_mcap_usd ∈ {25000, 30000, 40000, 50000}` to find the actual optimal floor before reverting.

### 3. Investigate the 2.2-min SL hold

Median SL exit in 2.2 minutes indicates entry timing is still poor — the bot is buying into short pumps. Options:
- Add a post-signal cooldown (e.g. require ≥30s since token hit the trending list before entry).
- Add `liquidityChange` slope gate: only enter if 5m liquidity is flat or rising (already in the LLM prompt as a signal but not in the filter layer).
- Check whether entry price is systematically 5-10% above the trending-list price due to poll lag.

### 4. Confirm catastrophic SL count is zero

Yesterday's rug filters were designed to cut the ≤-90% tail. Verify: query `SELECT COUNT(*), AVG(pnl_percent) FROM dry_run_positions WHERE status='closed' AND pnl_percent <= -50 AND closed_at_ms >= (EXTRACT(EPOCH FROM NOW())::BIGINT - 86400)*1000`. If any ≤-50% exits remain, inspect their snapshots for which GMGN field slipped through.

### 5. Hold trailing_percent at 15 — do not tighten

TRAILING_TP winners average +24.16% at 9.3 min hold. Tightening `trailing_percent` below 15 would reduce the lock price on every winner. The compression from +73% to +24% is already from the arm change, not the trail width — don't compound it.

### Breakeven math

At current magnitudes (SL avg -19.3%, TRAILING_TP avg +24.2%), breakeven win rate = 19.3 / (19.3 + 24.2) = **~44%**. Today's 34% is still below that. To close the gap without changing exit parameters:
- Either raise win rate 10pp via entry filters (recs 2 and 3 above), or
- Shrink avg SL fill from -19% toward -12% (faster trail arm or entry timing), or
- Widen avg TRAILING_TP from +24% toward +30% (lower the arm so more small wins accumulate).

Config stability recommendation: **no changes to exit logic today**. Let the 2026-05-18 config run one clean window without drift before tuning further. Use tomorrow's eval to decide which lever to pull.
