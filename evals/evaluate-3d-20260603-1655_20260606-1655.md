3. **Win rate stability** — 53.1% over 3d is consistent directionally (no alarm). Flat TP at 30% should be cross-checked against a 7d or 30d window to confirm TRAILING_TP remains dominant; if the flat TP begins firing at high frequency, that signals a regime change worth investigating.

This window's data does not contain a per-mcap breakdown; run a targeted SQL against `snapshot#>>'{candidate,marketCapUsd}'` bins to confirm whether the 90-100k cohort is still underperforming before applying the tighter cap.
